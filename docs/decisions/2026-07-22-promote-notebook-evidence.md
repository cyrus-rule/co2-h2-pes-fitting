# Promote notebook evidence and freeze the validation request

## Decision

Move the mature v2/v3 analyses into versioned package workflows and immutable
research artifacts while retaining presentation v3 as provenance.

Promoted items are:

- the complete evaluation specification;
- the 158--200 D-optimal count and robustness study;
- deterministic off-grid model--model diagnostics;
- the exact 48-orientation/144-energy independent-validation request;
- the returned-label decision rule;
- coefficient derivative and leave-one-radius-out diagnostics;
- the 6.4--8.0 Cyrus--Rohan overlap protocol.

## Evidence boundaries

The design study is retrospective finite-grid evidence. Off-grid disagreement
has no truth labels. Only future electronic-structure values at the frozen
coordinates can provide independent validation. Radial splines in this
iteration are diagnostics, not the production join.

## Consequence

The repository now preserves both the promoted method and the evidence that
selected it. New labels, an authoritative basis fixture, production radial
joining, certified YUMI I/O, and downstream broadening calculations remain
external scientific dependencies.
