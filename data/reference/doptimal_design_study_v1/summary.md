# D-optimal count and robustness study

- First strict known-grid pass: 161 orientations.
- The 180-point baseline safety factor is 3.02759.
- 180-point single-substitution pass rate: 97.2222%.
- Targeted label-free reoptimization recovered 16 of 16 observed 175--182 failures.
- The minimum 180-point safety factor over 36 scoring specifications is 1.5138.

These are retrospective finite-grid results. They support 180 as a robust planning choice, not as a mathematically optimal or independently validated ab initio budget.
